Hi, I’m Brenden Matthews. I’m a software engineer, author, artist, and
entrepreneur. I’ve worked with startups—three of which went on to pass a $1B
valuation—and built my own ventures.

I’m from Canada and have lived and worked in San Francisco and New York. Here I
write essays on engineering, entrepreneurship, and creative work, along with the
occasional journal entry.

Say hello or ask a question at
[hifromyourblog@brenden.brndn.io](mailto:hifromyourblog@brenden.brndn.io). No
marketing, please.
